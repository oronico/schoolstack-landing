FAQ

Common questions

What can I use today?

Three tools: Space for community intelligence and facilities, Budget for five-year financial planning, and Access for funding and policy guidance. Each works on its own and is free to use today.

Which schools is SchoolStack for?

SchoolStack is built for schools of 20 to 250 students. Space, Budget, and Access are open to any school.

We use another accounting platform. Can we still join?

Yes. SchoolStack runs on QuickBooks Online, and our accounting partner can move your books there. Email allison@schoolstack.ai to start.

What does it cost?

Space, Budget, and Access cost nothing to use today. Pricing for the 2027 SchoolStack platform has not been finalized.

What happens in 2027?

Our connected platform is in development for 2027. Space and Budget will connect with it, and Access will remain a stand-alone guide. We will explain any account or data changes before launch.

Does SchoolStack replace QuickBooks or Gusto?

No. QuickBooks Online remains your accounting system of record, and SchoolStack does not process payroll. Our QuickBooks Online integration starts rolling out with design partner schools in November 2026 and opens more widely as the platform does in 2027. A Gusto integration is planned.

Which QuickBooks Online plan do I need?

SchoolStack does not require QuickBooks Online Advanced. If your school is a nonprofit, review whether Advanced fits your needs: its additional categorization options can help when you prepare your Form 990.
