FAQ

Common questions

What can I use today?

All four: Space for facilities and community, Budget for financial modeling, Access for funding information, and Lending Lab for financing. No need to wait for the platform.

Is it free?

The tools are free to use today. Philanthropic partners fund our work so we can focus on getting it right, and we intend to keep SchoolStack BI within reach of the schools that need it most.

Does using a tool mean I can get a loan?

No. Space, Budget, and Access are planning and information tools, and none of them decide financing. Lending Lab is a separate pathway with real underwriting, and its screener tells you whether you are a fit.

Is my data safe?

School data deserves real care. We follow FERPA expectations for student information, encrypt sensitive data at rest, and are working toward SOC 2. Ask us for specifics and we will tell you plainly where we are.
