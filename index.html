FAQ

Common questions

What can I use today?

All four tools: Space for facilities and community, Budget for five-year financial planning, Access for funding and policy guidance, and Lending Lab for financing. Each works on its own.

What does it cost?

The four tools available today cost nothing to use. Pricing for the 2027 SchoolStack platform has not been finalized. QuickBooks Online Advanced is a separate paid subscription.

What happens in 2027?

Space, Budget, and Lending Lab will connect with the SchoolStack platform. Access will remain a stand-alone guide. We will explain any account or data changes before launch.

What does checking my Lending Lab fit tell me?

It provides an initial indication of whether the school may fit the program and what may need to be strengthened. It is not loan approval. Human underwriting and a credit decision come later.
