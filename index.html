How many students do you need to break even?

Enter what a year of running your school costs and what each student brings in. The answer is your enrollment driver: the number the rest of your budget leans on.

$

60 students

$600,000 ÷ $10,000 per student

At 60 students you collect $600,000, exactly what a year costs.

Every student past this number adds to your cushion. Enrollment below it means a gap to close with fundraising, grants or lower costs.

Estimates are for planning only and are not loan eligibility. The break-even count assumes every enrolled student pays the amount entered and leaves out grants and fundraising.

FAQ

Common questions

What can I use today?

Three tools: Space for community intelligence and facilities, Budget for five-year financial planning, and Access for funding and policy guidance. Each works on its own and is free to use today.

What does it cost?

Space, Budget, and Access cost nothing to use today. Pricing for the 2027 SchoolStack platform has not been finalized.

What happens in 2027?

Our connected platform is in development for 2027. Space and Budget will connect with it, and Access will remain a stand-alone guide. We will explain any account or data changes before launch.

Does SchoolStack replace QuickBooks or Gusto?

No. QuickBooks Online remains your accounting system of record, and SchoolStack does not process payroll. A QuickBooks Online integration and a Gusto integration are planned for the connected platform. Neither is live yet, and you will not need QuickBooks Online Advanced.
