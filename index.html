FAQ

Common questions

What can I use today?

All four: Space for facilities and community, Budget for financial modeling, Access for funding information, and Lending Lab for financing. No need to wait for the platform.

Is it free?

Yes. Every MVP feature above is free between now and the November launch. Philanthropic partners fund our work so we can focus on getting it right, and we intend to keep SchoolStack BI within reach of the schools that need it most.

What happens to Space and Budget in November?

They grow into it. Both were built to meet an immediate need, and in November they become part of the full accounting and enrollment platform rather than staying separate tools. Access and Lending Lab stay where they are. We will walk you through what changes well before the launch.

What does prequalifying tell me?

Where you stand today and what to strengthen before you apply. Real underwriting comes after, done by people who read school finances for a living. Space, Budget, and Access are there to help you plan and prepare.

Is my data safe?

School data deserves real care. We follow FERPA expectations for student information, encrypt sensitive data at rest, and are working toward SOC 2. Ask us for specifics and we will tell you plainly where we are.
