page on 25 November 2026 ("Remember the YC checklist"). Check every change
to `index.html` against it, alongside CLAUDE.md. Where the two disagree,
CLAUDE.md wins: no fake proof, no invented screenshots, "free" only with

## The six sections, top to bottom

1. **Hero.** The headline, who it is for, the offer and the main action
   all sit on the first screen. Show the product there too: a demo or a
   short preview, not hidden below the fold. Short sentences, concrete
   words. Say what they get, not how new it is.
2. **Problem and transformation.** Tell a before and after: the painful
   old way, then a better day with the product. Name the pains in the
   reader's own words, then tie each one to what the product does.
3. **Social proof.** Real people with names, faces and roles. Logos of
   those who use it. Real numbers you can stand behind. Never stock
   photos, anonymous quotes or invented figures.
4. **Pricing.** Show the price. YC's advice is three tiers with the one
   you want chosen in the middle, marked Most Popular.
5. **FAQ.** A short list of the objections people really raise, answered
   in the brand's own voice. Not a dumping ground for the rest of the page.
6. **Footer.** Terms and Privacy, comparison pages against the
   alternatives a reader is weighing, and links to free tools.

## Common mistakes

- Offer, demo or main action below the first screen.
- Hero copy that sounds big and says nothing.
- Features with no before and after, and no named pain.
- Fake proof.
- One unclear plan, or a long menu of plans with none marked.
- An FAQ that reads like a second website or a policy document.
- A footer with no Terms, Privacy, comparisons or free tools.

## How it applies to SchoolStack

- **Pricing does not apply yet.** Space, Budget and Access are free, and
  platform pricing will be released in 2027 (owner, 25 November 2026). Do
  not build tiers or name a price before then (CLAUDE.md section 7).
- **Proof must be real.** Design partners started in November 2026, so
  there is no school quote yet. Until one exists, the proof is the
  founder (a real person with a name, a face and a role) and the funders.
  Never write a quote, a logo or a number nobody gave us.
- **The product shown must be real.** The platform film is real footage;
  a still from it is the only image of the platform the hero may use
  (CLAUDE.md section 6: never invent a screenshot).
