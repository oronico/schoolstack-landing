A checklist for school leaders

Get ready for SchoolStack

Seven things to gather before your first day on SchoolStack. Each one pays off on its own: with your board, your bookkeeper and any lender you talk to.

See the checklist Build your budget

The checklist

  1. 1. Your books in QuickBooks OnlineSchoolStack runs on QuickBooks Online, your system of record. Advanced is not required; for a nonprofit, review whether its extra categories help with Form 990. On Desktop, Wave or spreadsheets? Our accounting partner can move your books: allison@schoolstack.ai.
  2. 2. Books reconciled through last monthEvery bank and card account matches its statement through the last full month.
  3. 3. A chart of accounts that tells your money apartTuition, each ESA, voucher or scholarship program, grants and donations each get their own income account, so you see what arrives when.
  4. 4. A current budgetThis year and the next four in SchoolStack Budget, with your break-even: the students it takes to cover a year.
  5. 5. A roster with each student’s fundingOne row per student: grade, tuition, any discount, and who pays: the family, an ESA, a voucher or a scholarship.
  6. 6. Last year’s financial statementsBalance sheet, profit and loss, and cash flow statement for your last full fiscal year, from QuickBooks or your bookkeeper.
  7. 7. Your leaseRent, the yearly increase and who pays for what, so the budget carries the real cost of your space.

General information for planning, not accounting or tax advice. Check with your bookkeeper or CPA.
