Privacy policy

Last updated November 2026

The short version: this site sets no cookies, runs no advertising or tracking scripts, and never sells your details. We collect what you choose to send us, and the basic records any web host keeps.

Who we are

SchoolStack is a program of Building Hope Impact Fund, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. In this policy, “we” means SchoolStack and Building Hope Impact Fund. This policy covers schoolstack.ai. SchoolStack Space, Budget, and Access are separate sites with their own privacy terms, so please read those when you use them.

What we collect

When you join the early list

The early list form asks for:

  • Your first name
  • Your work email
  • Your school or organization name
  • Your state
  • The accounting platform you use, if you tell us

Submissions are stored with Netlify, the company that hosts this site, through its form service.

When you email us

We keep your message and your email address so we can reply.

When you visit

Like any web host, Netlify records basic information about each request to the site, such as your IP address, browser type, the page you asked for, the site that referred you, and the time. We use these records to keep the site running and secure, and to count which pages and links people use, including clicks on the buttons that open Space, Budget, and Access.

When you use the durability snapshot

The numbers you enter stay in your browser. They are not sent to us or stored anywhere.

When you use Enrollment Audit

Your answers are saved in your browser’s own storage on your device, so you can come back to them. They are not sent to us. The tool’s “Clear all answers” button removes them.

What we do not do

  • We do not set cookies on this site.
  • We do not run advertising, tracking, or outside analytics scripts.
  • We do not sell or rent your information.

How we use your information

  • To tell people on the early list when early access opens to them, and send them product updates.
  • To send news and event invitations to anyone who joined our earlier mailing list.
  • To answer your messages.
  • To keep the site secure and understand how it is used.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the services that help us run SchoolStack, such as our web host and email provider, and only so they can do that work for us. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Every product email has a way to unsubscribe, and you can ask us to stop at any time.
  • You can ask to see, correct, or delete the information you sent us.

Email allison@schoolstack.ai for any of these.

How long we keep it

We keep early list details until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove them, and the same goes for an address given to our earlier mailing list. We keep other contact details only while we need them to reply, and delete them when you ask.

Children

This site is for adults who run or support schools. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Security

The site is served only over HTTPS. Early list sign-ups are kept in our host’s form service, not in the site’s code.

Links to other sites

This site links to sites we do not run, such as Instagram, LinkedIn, and our partners. Their own privacy policies apply there.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update it here and change the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy: allison@schoolstack.ai
