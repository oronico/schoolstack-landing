Built around the people building schools.

You’re building a school where children are known by name and families feel they belong. Behind that work are consequential business decisions, often made with a small team and limited support.

SchoolStack brings together practical digital tools and experienced human support to help small-school leaders understand their finances, navigate facility decisions, and build relationships in their communities.

We believe understanding your business should give you more confidence, more options, and more time for the people who count on you.

Incubated at Building Hope Impact Fund

SchoolStack is a program of Building Hope Impact Fund, an operating nonprofit that develops and grows initiatives supporting education entrepreneurs.

The Impact Fund provides a home for SchoolStack’s development as we build alongside school leaders. Our work draws on the broader Building Hope organization’s experience helping schools navigate financing, facilities, and operations.

  • SchoolStack is a program of Building Hope Impact Fund.
  • Building Hope Impact Fund is SchoolStack’s incubator.
  • School Harbor is the technology development partner, led for SchoolStack by Daniel Crook.
The team

Meet the people behind SchoolStack

Allison Serafin

  • Founder, SchoolStack
  • Chief of Strategy & Development, Building Hope

Allison brings more than 20 years of experience across teaching, education policy, philanthropy, and school development.

She began her career in a Houston classroom, founded Opportunity180 in Las Vegas, and served as an elected member and vice president of the Nevada State Board of Education. At IDEA Public Schools, she helped raise more than $40 million and led real estate strategy and site acquisition for its Houston expansion. She later managed the domestic philanthropic portfolio at Dovetail Impact Foundation.

At Building Hope, Allison works with school leaders navigating the financial and facility decisions that shape their futures. She founded SchoolStack to make practical business tools and trusted support more accessible to small schools.

Beyond SchoolStack: Allison lives in Summerville, South Carolina, where she helps care for her parents and has two dogs. She loves a good book, time near the water, and exploring what AI can make possible.

Daniel Crook

Our values

What guides us

Our values shape how we build, how we work together, and how we serve schools.

  • Love thy neighbor.

    Know people by name. Understand what matters to them. Show up with care and put our knowledge, relationships, and resources to work for others.

  • Be curious.

    Ask thoughtful questions. Listen beyond the first answer. Challenge assumptions, including our own, and let what we learn shape what we build.

  • Respect and humility.

    Honor the experience and dignity each person brings. Welcome feedback, share credit, and own our mistakes. Keep our commitments and earn trust through our actions.

  • Clear is kind.

    Tell the truth with care. Make the complicated understandable. Be clear about what we can deliver, what needs to change, and what comes next.

  • Radical optimism.

    See possibility. Face reality. Believe better is possible and do the work to make it happen. Bring hope, resourcefulness, and discipline to the hardest problems.
